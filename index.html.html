A Main Street fixture

Kountry Korner has been a stop for locals and travelers passing through the New River Gorge area, run out of a low-key storefront right on Main Street. It's the kind of place where the deli case does the talking: homemade chili and slaw for your hot dog, chicken salad and ham salad made fresh, and a rotation of BBQ and comfort-food plates.

Alongside the food counter, Kountry Korner keeps a corner of the store stocked with handmade crafts, so a stop in for lunch usually turns into a slower look around. Regulars come back for the pepperoni rolls and the meatloaf when it's on.

The store is casual, kid-friendly, and set up for an easy stop: free street parking, an on-site lot, and a wheelchair-accessible car park out front.
